Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DevSpark ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

1. Information We Collect

We only receive the information you choose to send us, such as your name, email address, selected service and project details when you use our enquiry form, WhatsApp or email. We may also receive files and links you share so we can complete your project.

2. How the Enquiry Form Works

Our form does not store your details on a server. It opens WhatsApp or your email app with your message pre-filled, and the message reaches us only when you send it.

3. How We Use Information

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote
  • To deliver, support and improve our services
  • To send project updates and invoices
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with tools and service providers needed to run your project (for example file storage, payment or messaging platforms) or when required by law.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

This website does not use advertising cookies. Your browser may store your light/dark theme choice. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy.

6. Data Retention and Security

We keep project information only as long as needed for the work, support and legal records. We use reasonable measures to protect your data, but no online method is completely secure.

7. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing hello@devspark.com.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to social media or other sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

10. Contact

Questions? Email hello@devspark.com.
