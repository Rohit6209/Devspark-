Refund Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

We want you to be happy with every project. This policy explains when refunds and revisions apply to DevSpark services. All prices are in USD.

1. Revisions First

Every project includes revisions. If something is not right, tell us and we will work with you to fix it before any refund is considered.

2. Eligibility for a Refund

  • Before work starts: full refund of the amount paid
  • After work has started but before the first draft: refund of the amount paid minus the work already completed
  • After the first draft has been delivered: partial refund only, based on the work completed and agreed between both parties
  • After final delivery and approval: no refund

3. Non-Refundable Items

Third-party costs already paid on your behalf (such as domains, hosting, stock media or licences) and custom work that has been delivered and approved are not refundable. Monthly services (maintenance, SEO) can be cancelled before the next billing cycle; the current month is not refunded.

4. Delays

If we miss an agreed deadline for reasons within our control and cannot deliver within a reasonable extension, you may request a refund for the undelivered work.

5. How to Request a Refund

Email hello@devspark.com with your name, service and reason within 7 days of the issue. We review requests within 5 business days.

6. Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Timing depends on your bank or payment provider.

7. Contact

Email hello@devspark.com or message us on WhatsApp.
